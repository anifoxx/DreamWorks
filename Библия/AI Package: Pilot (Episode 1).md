# AI Package: Pilot (Episode 1)

This AI Package provides prompts and notes for generating the keyframes and video shots described in
the Shot List. For each shot we define a GPT Image prompt (to create a still frame reference), a
negative prompt (to guide the model away from undesired elements), a Veo prompt (to animate the shot
from the reference), continuation notes for linking shots, motion instructions, a continuity
checklist and rendering notes. Prompts focus on realism, consistent character appearance and
adherence to the Visual Bible.

## Conventions

- Characters: Vanessa has long dark hair, green eyes, old-money style; Leo has russet hair, blue eyes and a calm demeanour. Their appearances should be consistent with the Character Bible.

- Environment: The balcony scene is set at night with a view of Moscow’s lights; the kitchen scene is modern and bright.

- Style: Cinematic realism, shallow depth for close-ups, handheld feel, Kodak Vision3 500T film emulation.

- Negative prompt keywords: avoid cartoonish faces, distortions, exaggerated emotions, glamorous fashion, unrealistic lighting, obvious CGI.

## Shot-by-Shot Prompts

## Shot 1 – Establishing Shot (Balcony)

- GPT Image prompt: “Wide establishing shot of a modern Moscow high-rise balcony at night. City lights shimmer in the distance. The balcony railing and table with a candle are silhouetted. 35 mm lens, cinematic realism, deep blue night sky, warm candle glow, natural film grain.”

- Negative prompt: “No cartoon styles, no HDR, no futuristic skyscrapers, no people in frame, no

## glossy textures.”

- Veo prompt: “Start with the still frame of the wide balcony at night. Hold for a moment, then slowly pan right to reveal more of the skyline. Maintain a 35 mm lens perspective and keep the

## candle flicker and city lights. Duration 4 s.”

- Continuation: This shot fades into the medium shot of Vanessa (Shot 2) with a simple cut.

- Motion notes: Pan is smooth and slow; no zooming. Camera is on a tripod.

- Continuity checklist: Verify night sky tone; candle present; no characters; matching skyline with World Bible.

- Rendering notes: Use a film grain overlay and Kodak Vision3 500T LUT. Resolution 1920×1080 (vertical crop in post). Keep shadows deep and colour temperature cool.

## Shot 2 – Vanessa Medium (Balcony)

- GPT Image prompt: “Medium shot of a young woman standing at a balcony railing at night. She has long dark hair and wears an elegant old-money coat draped around her shoulders. She looks out at the city. Warm candlelight illuminates her profile while cool blue city lights rim her hair. 50 mm lens, realistic cinematic lighting.”

- Negative prompt: “No smiling or exaggerated poses, no heavy makeup, no futuristic outfits, no

## studio lighting.”

- Veo prompt: “Begin with the reference image of Vanessa gazing at the city. Use a handheld push-in towards her over 4 seconds, capturing the subtle movement of her hair in the night breeze. Maintain 50 mm lens and shallow depth of field; candle and city lights should flicker

## naturally.”

- Continuation: Seamlessly cut to shot 3 as Leo enters. Vanessa maintains position and gaze.

- Motion notes: Handheld sway mimics breathing. Ensure push-in speed is constant.

- Continuity checklist: Vanessa’s hair down and coat draped correctly; candle remains lit; city lights consistent with Shot 1.

- Rendering notes: Maintain film grain; preserve highlight roll-off; no sudden brightness shifts.

## Shot 3 – Leo Entrance (Balcony)

- GPT Image prompt: “Medium shot of a handsome man with russet hair carrying two mugs stepping out onto a balcony at night. He wears a casual yet stylish sweater and jeans. The railing and a small table are visible; the city skyline glows behind him. 50 mm lens, warm candlelight,

## cinematic realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No smirking, no exaggerated swagger, no plastic textures, no futuristic

## clothing.”

- Veo prompt: “Animate Leo entering from the apartment doorway into the balcony space. The camera (steadycam) follows him in a smooth arc towards Vanessa. Use a 50 mm lens with shallow depth of field; keep the candle flicker and city backdrop. Duration 5 s.”

- Continuation: Leads into shot 4 where Leo stands beside Vanessa.

- Motion notes: Smooth steadicam; slight parallax as camera arcs around the balcony table.

- Continuity checklist: Both mugs should be identical; Leo’s hair neat; clothing matches Character Bible; city view matches previous shots.

- Rendering notes: Balanced exposure; warm tones on faces; cool bokeh in background.

## Shot 4 – Two-Shot (Balcony)

- GPT Image prompt: “Two elegant people stand side by side on a balcony at night, each holding a mug. The man gazes softly at the woman; she looks down shyly. Warm candlelight illuminates their faces while the Moscow skyline blurs behind them. 50 mm lens, cinematic realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No dramatic backlighting, no glamorous fashion, no visible camera crew, no

## lens flares.”

- Veo prompt: “Start from the reference image of both characters on the balcony. Perform a slow dolly inwards for 4 s while the actors take small sips and exchange glances. Keep a 50 mm lens; handheld breathing motion. Warm tones should dominate the foreground with cool bokeh in

## the background.”

- Continuation: Cuts to close-ups (shots 5–6) to emphasise dialogue and emotion.

- Motion notes: Dolly speed slow; maintain equal distance to both actors.

- Continuity checklist: Both mugs remain nearly full; candle stays on table; hair, wardrobe consistent; skyline unchanged.

- Rendering notes: Ensure natural skin tones; keep depth of field shallow; maintain film grain.

## Shot 5 – Close-up on Leo (Balcony)

- GPT Image prompt: “Close-up of a handsome man in his 30s speaking softly at night. He has russet hair, blue eyes and a calm expression. Warm candlelight illuminates his features; the background is a dark bokeh of city lights. 85 mm lens, shallow depth of field, cinematic realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No harsh shadows, no over-sharpening, no unnatural eye reflections.”

- Veo prompt: “Hold the close-up of Leo delivering his heartfelt speech for 5 s. The camera remains static with micro-jitters to emulate handheld. Maintain an 85 mm lens; ensure the light

## source flickers subtly on his face.”

- Continuation: After his line, cut to shot 6 for Vanessa’s reaction.

- Motion notes: Micro-jitters only; no rack focus.

- Continuity checklist: Leo’s face hair trimmed as per Character Bible; consistent background bokeh; candle light warm.

- Rendering notes: Apply subtle film grain; maintain warm midtones.

## Shot 6 – Close-up on Vanessa (Balcony)

- GPT Image prompt: “Close-up of a young woman listening intently at night. She has dark hair and green eyes; her expression shifts between hope and doubt. Warm candlelight highlights her features; cool city lights rim the frame. 85 mm lens, cinematic realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No tears, no smiling, no cinematic bloom, no lens distortion.”

- Veo prompt: “Hold the close-up of Vanessa reacting for 5 s. The camera is static with subtle handheld sway. Maintain 85 mm lens and shallow depth of field. Her eyes flicker as she

## processes Leo’s words.”

- Continuation: Crossfade to shot 7 (insert of cups) as conversation ends.

- Motion notes: Slight breathing movement; ensure eyes stay in focus.

- Continuity checklist: Hair parting consistent; make-up natural; candlelight flicker.

- Rendering notes: Balanced highlights; maintain warm key light and cool rim.

## Shot 7 – Insert of Cups (Night–Morning Transition)

- GPT Image prompt: “Close overhead view of two mugs on a balcony table at night. A small candle burns beside them. The mugs are nearly full. The city skyline is blurred in the background. 35 mm lens, cinematic realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No human figures, no messy table, no neon colours.”

- Veo prompt: “Begin with the reference image of the cups at night. Hold for 2 s, then slowly dissolve to the same composition at dawn. The liquid in the cups is unchanged. Maintain 35 mm

## lens and static camera. Duration 5 s.”

- Continuation: Dissolve into shot 8 establishing the kitchen.

- Motion notes: Use dissolve transition to convey passage of time.

- Continuity checklist: Cup positions unchanged; candle may have burned down slightly; mugs match props list.

- Rendering notes: Ensure seamless blend between night and morning colour temperatures. Night image uses cool blue shadows; morning uses soft warm daylight.

## Shot 8 – Establishing Shot (Kitchen)

- GPT Image prompt: “Wide vertical shot of a modern, open-plan kitchen in morning light. Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the space with cool daylight. Two cups from the previous night sit on a wooden table. Sleek cabinets and a marble island are visible. 35 mm lens, cinematic

## realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No people, no clutter, no harsh shadows, no trendy neon.”

- Veo prompt: “Start with the still frame of the empty kitchen at dawn. Hold for 2 s, then gently push in towards the table for 4 s, drawing attention to the cups. Use a 35 mm lens and tripod. Natural morning light shifts slightly as the sun rises.”

- Continuation: Cut to shot 9 as Leo enters.

- Motion notes: Slow push-in; emphasise quiet morning atmosphere.

- Continuity checklist: Cups unchanged from balcony; window view matches World Bible; lighting consistent with time of day.

- Rendering notes: Colour temperature cool but softened; maintain film grain; highlights not blown.

## Shot 9 – Leo Cooking (Kitchen)

- GPT Image prompt: “Medium shot of a man with russet hair cooking breakfast in a modern kitchen. He wears a light sweater and jeans. The sun casts a cool morning glow through the windows while warm light from a pendant lamp illuminates him. 50 mm lens, cinematic realism.”

- Negative prompt: “No messy countertops, no futuristic appliances, no saturated colours.”

- Veo prompt: “Animate Leo cracking eggs and stirring food at the stove. The camera is handheld, with slight micro-movement as if by a person watching him from a distance. Use a 50 mm lens

## and maintain natural light interplay for 5 s.”

- Continuation: Cut to shot 10 as Vanessa enters.

- Motion notes: Subtle handheld; keep focus on Leo’s hands and face.

- Continuity checklist: Wardrobe, hair as per Character Bible; kitchen props in place; cups visible in background.

- Rendering notes: Combine cool daylight and warm stove light; maintain soft shadows.

## Shot 10 – Vanessa Enters (Kitchen)

- GPT Image prompt: “Medium shot of a young woman with long dark hair walking into a modern kitchen in morning light. She notices two cups on the table and smiles softly. 50 mm lens, realistic lighting.”

- Negative prompt: “No exaggerated smile, no hurried movement, no unrealistic costumes.”

- Veo prompt: “Animate Vanessa walking into the kitchen, pausing to glance at the cups, then moving toward the table. The camera (steadicam) follows from behind, panning to her side as she reacts. Use a 50 mm lens; duration 5 s.”

- Continuation: Cut to shot 11 for the breakfast conversation.

- Motion notes: Smooth follow; slight pan reveals her reaction.

- Continuity checklist: Vanessa’s hair and clothing consistent; cups positioned as in shot 8; morning light consistent.

- Rendering notes: Maintain natural color temperature; keep depth of field moderate to separate her from background.

## Shots 11–14 – Breakfast Dialogue and Close-ups

For the remaining shots (table conversation, close-ups and insert), follow similar structure:

- GPT Image prompts should describe two characters seated at a modern kitchen table in the morning, with warm and cool light interplay. Close-ups focus on faces, emphasising micro-expressions and subtle mood shifts.

- Negative prompts should avoid melodrama, theatrical lighting, glossy cinematography or fashion magazine aesthetics.

- Veo prompts should specify static or slight push-in camera, maintain lens consistency (50 mm for two-shot, 85 mm for close-ups), and indicate the duration (4–5 s) with natural pauses for dialogue delivery. For the final insert of the cups (shot 14), specify an overhead static shot and a slow fade out.

- Motion notes should highlight small movements: hand gestures, head tilts, eye contact.

- Continuity checklist should ensure props (cups, plates), wardrobe and hair remain consistent; emotional progression aligns with Episode Design.

- Rendering notes should maintain the established film stock look, natural skin tones and balanced lighting.

## General Notes

- All GPT Image prompts should be used to generate high-resolution keyframes that capture the composition, lighting and mood of each shot. These images become the reference for the Veo animations.

- Negative prompts explicitly exclude unwanted styles and ensure realism.

- Veo prompts should describe movement, lens, duration and mood succinctly. Use “start from the reference image” as a baseline.

- Continuity checklists should be consulted by the continuity supervisor before and after each take.

- Rendering notes remind the compositing and colour grading teams of the desired filmic look (Kodak Vision3 500T emulation) and aspect ratio (9:16 vertical for social media).
